Lesson 1 · The sun, our star

Our sun

The sun is a star. It is a big ball of hot gas.

The sun is the closest star to Earth. That is why it looks bigger and brighter than other stars.

We cannot live without heat and light from the sun.

But the sun can also harm us. It can give us a sunburn!

Lesson 2 · Earth rotation

Day and night

The Earth spins around. It never stops. This is called rotation.

It makes one whole spin every 24 hours.

The part of Earth facing the sun has daytime. The part facing away from the sun has nighttime.
