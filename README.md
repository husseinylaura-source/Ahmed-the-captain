# Ahmed's Sky Adventure

An interactive Year 1 science website made for Ahmed, based on **Topic 3: Sky and Earth**
(Lesson 1: Observe the Sky · Lesson 2: Patterns in the Sky · Standard 1.ESS.1.1).

## What's inside
| Chapter | Ahmed learns | Activities |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Sky objects | Sun, moon and stars; day–night pattern | Tap-to-reveal cards, 5 questions |
| 2 Our sun | The sun is a star, helpful vs harmful, the planets | Tap the planets, 6 questions |
| 3 Moon & gravity | Moon is rock, reflects light, orbits Earth; gravity pulls down | Gravity playground, 5 questions |
| 4 Day & night | Earth rotation, 24 hours, facing / away from the sun | Spin the Earth with Ahmed on it, "Day or night at the X?" |
| 5 Moon phases | New, crescent, quarter, gibbous, full | Moon-orbit slider, picture questions |
| 6 Big quiz | Everything | 10 random questions + certificate |

**Read-aloud:** every sentence and question has a blue speaker button, and the "Read to me"
switch reads each new question automatically. Words light up as they are read.
It uses the browser's built-in voice (works best in Chrome, Edge or Safari, including iPad).

Stars, progress and the chosen chapter are saved in the browser.

## Files
- `index.html` – the whole website (HTML, CSS and JavaScript in one file)
- `ahmed.jpg` – Ahmed's photo (used in the header, the start screen, on the spinning Earth and in the cheers)

## Put it on GitHub Pages
1. Create a new repository on github.com (for example `ahmed-sky-adventure`).
2. Click **Add file → Upload files** and upload `index.html`, `ahmed.jpg` and `README.md`. Commit.
3. Go to **Settings → Pages**. Under *Build and deployment* choose **Deploy from a branch**, branch `main`, folder `/ (root)`. Save.
4. After a minute the site is live at `https://<your-username>.github.io/ahmed-sky-adventure/`.

> Privacy: a public repository and a GitHub Pages site can be seen by anyone with the link,
> including Ahmed's photo. To keep the photo private, replace `ahmed.jpg` with a drawing or
> avatar before publishing.

## Change the name or photo
Replace `ahmed.jpg` with another square photo, then search `index.html` for `Ahmed` and change it.
